[Paystack’s](https://paystack.com) sweeping goal is to help African businesses get paid by anyone, anywhere in the world. In particular, **Paystack Commerce** is a toolkit for African creators to bring ideas to market seamlessly. It’s easy to set up, and creators can begin selling their products with just a few steps.

---

## Initial Thoughts

Paystack Commerce has been around for a while. It was launched over a year ago to much fanfare and has seen decent traction. My approach here is to:

1. Define the goal of the proposed improvements.  
2. Identify relevant user segments.  
3. Prioritize one segment and propose solutions.  
4. Focus on a solution and walk through the thought process.  
5. Summarize and conclude.

---

## Goals and Metrics

We should focus on improving **Acquisition, Engagement, and Retention**:

- **Acquisition** → How many creators are using Paystack Commerce? Can we attract more?  
- **Engagement** → How engaged are creators? Can we deepen their interaction?  
- **Monetisation** → Are there opportunities to earn more per creator?  

Given the product’s stage, monetisation isn’t the priority. Instead, acquisition should be the focus.

---

## User Segments

- **Creators selling physical goods** (fashion, gadgets).  
- **Event creators** (tickets, live events).  
- **Digital product creators** (books, courses, exclusive content).  

For this improvement, I prioritize **creators selling physical goods**. This segment is large, has massive potential, and lacks excellent solutions.

---

## Proposed Solutions

### 1. Marketplace
Aggregate all Paystack Commerce stores into a **Discover section**. Creators opt-in to feature items, using this as a customer acquisition channel.

### 2. Facebook Ad Integration
Enable creators to promote their storefronts directly from Paystack.  
- Link FB accounts.  
- Boost pages/items.  
- Manage ads and funding.  
- View analytics within Paystack.  

This solution aligns with Paystack’s mission and vision, helping creators grow sales seamlessly.

### 3. Offer Discounts
Creators can generate coupon codes to incentivize purchases.

### 4. Affiliate Marketing Program
Creators create affiliate links, and affiliates earn a cut per sale. This drives demand and expands reach.

---

## Prioritizing a Solution

Using parameters like **impact, effort/complexity, and confidence**, Facebook Ad Integration stands out. It directly supports Paystack Commerce’s vision and increases the likelihood that businesses will get paid.

---

## Flow

**Grow your sales!**  
Set up your store and reach thousands instantly by activating FB ads directly from your dashboard.

Key components:  
- Link FB account.  
- Promote (page/item).  
- Add targeting and audience details.  
- Manage ads and funding.  
- View analytics and performance.

---

## Metrics

- **North Star Metric** → Number of payment pages promoted within the first month.  
- Proxy metrics:  
  - Page visits from Facebook.  
  - Products sold via Facebook.  
  - Total GMV of products sold via Facebook.

---

## Conclusion

Paystack Commerce helps African businesses get paid, but creators struggle with growth. By enabling **Facebook Ad Integration**, Paystack can help creators push their businesses in front of thousands of prospective buyers
